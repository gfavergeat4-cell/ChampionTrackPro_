What Body Language Doesn't Show

"When you ask a player how they feel, they look you in the eyes and say 'I'm good, coach,' because they want to play."

You read body language as well as anyone — that's not in question. But body language shows what's already visible. It doesn't show the CNS fatigue building underneath, the week before it becomes a hamstring.
