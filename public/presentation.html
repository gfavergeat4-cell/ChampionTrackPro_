Proof of Concept

Cesson-Rennes Handball
Starligue 2025–26

The situation

Second-to-last in France's top professional league. 6 months without a win. Recurring match-day injuries. No sports scientist. Every decision made on intuition alone.

The intervention

I tracked 13 performance indicators across 3 categories — physical, technical, psychological — after every session. Weekly reports with concrete training recommendations based on what players reported feeling, not what was visible on the court.

Key finding — week 2: Match load was the highest physical and technical demand of the week — higher than any training session. Players were being under-prepared in practice for what they faced on game day. I recommended simulating match intensity at least once per week in practice. Implemented immediately.

Each player is compared only against her own 28-day baseline — never against teammates. A score of 55 on a player whose baseline is 80 is a signal. The same score on a player whose baseline is 50 is normal. That's the difference.
